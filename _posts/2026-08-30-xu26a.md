title: Conformalized Time Series Anomaly Thresholding with Latent Space Features
abstract: Time series anomaly detection is an important task applicable to a wide
  range of fields. Many methods have been proposed to address this task, but evaluation
  often suffers leakage from flawed thresholding methods. In this paper we explore
  leveraging latent space features in a conformal framework to automatically determine
  alarm thresholds without the risk of leakage and reducing false alarm rates. We
  show that our method is able to match or surpass the performance of a popular problematic
  thresholding method while using only information from the calibration set.
