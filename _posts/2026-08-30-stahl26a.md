title: Weighted Conformalized Quantile Regression for Active Learning
abstract: A common approach in active learning is to improve models by acquiring samples
  for which the prediction is uncertain (Ren et al., 2021). Conformal quantile regression
  seems like a perfect match for estimating such prediction uncertainties, but any
  acquisition strategy used in the active learning setting would introduce selection
  and hence, violate the exchangeability assumption on which conformal prediction
  relies on. This paper addresses the challenge of maintaining valid uncertainty quantification
  in active learning for regression by utilizing Weighted Conformalized Quantile Regression
  with a Weighted CV+ approach. This restores predictive guarantees by re-weighting
  nonconformity scores based on the likelihood ratio between training and test distributions.
  Experimental results on the California Housing dataset demonstrate that uncertainty-based
  acquisition strategies accelerate error reduction (MSE), and that the weighting
  to maintain target coverage is required in order to keep the guarantees. Furthermore,
  the analysis identifies that there is a risk that greedy acquisition functions over-sample
  regions of irreducible noise, and demonstrates that a stochastic weighted strategy
  effectively mitigates this risk while balancing manifold exploration with predictive
  precision.
