title: 'ConfBatch: Conformal Prediction for Adaptive Batch Sizing'
abstract: We study whether conformal uncertainty can guide adaptive batch size selection
  during neural network training. We propose ConfBatch that periodically computes
  conformal prediction sets on held-out data, using their size as an uncertainty score
  to adjust batch size. On CIFAR-10, the opposite-direction ConfBatch variants achieve
  the highest accuracies, while standard ConfBatch has the lowest wall time among
  adaptive batch-size methods.
