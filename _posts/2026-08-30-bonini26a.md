title: Geometry-Aware Conformal Decoding for Motor BCI Systems
abstract: Brain-computer interface (BCI) motor decoders need accurate kinematic commands
  and reliable uncertainty. In closed loop, velocity uncertainty can gate or scale
  commands or give users feedback. Prior non-invasive BCI work used conformal prediction
  to defer uncertain discrete exoskeleton commands (Eliades and Papadopoulos, 2019);
  invasive work remains limited to one fixed-width global interval around 1D reach
  direction or position (Wei et al., 2024). We instead compare complete decoder-geometry
  systems, not geometries around a common predictor, for continuous 2D velocity decoding.
